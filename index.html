Installing on Android

  1. Open this page on the phone and tap Download APK (or scan the QR code from a computer).
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unrecognised app, choose Install anyway — these are test builds, not from the Play Store.
